# External inspiration and source links

This skill is an original, client-agnostic synthesis of the user's requirements and publicly described practices. These references explain ideas to **adapt**, not code to embed or templates to redistribute. Preserve respective licenses and trademarks if incorporating any actual third-party materials.

## 1. Academic PPTX (Gabberflast)

- Repository: https://github.com/Gabberflast/academic-pptx-skill
- Useful ideas: statement/action titles, ghost-deck test, one insight per slide, exhibit discipline, citations, communication-first QA.
- Deliberate adaptations: commercial action and decision framing, meaningful comparisons, on-slide commentary beyond the exhibit, shorter C-suite body, client-approved branding and speaker-note separation.
- Do not impose the academic skill's fixed colors, citation-slide format, font sizes or one-exhibit restriction on executive decks. Its repo lists an MIT license, but check its files' terms before copying any verbatim implementation.

## 2. think-cell / Minto Pyramid Principle

- https://www.think-cell.com/en/blog/using-the-pyramid-principle-to-build-better-powerpoint-presentations
- Conclusions first, then distinct supporting arguments and evidence. Align slide headlines with the deck's logical hierarchy; use reverse storyboarding.

## 3. IBCS - International Business Communication Standards

- https://www.ibcs.com/IBCS/
- https://www.ibcs.com/standards/page/4/
- Business communication norms for conveying a message, consistent semantic notation, accurate visual comparisons, reducing noise and avoiding distortions. Adapt at the principle level; do not claim this custom skill is formally IBCS-certified.

## 4. Duarte - executive/data storytelling

- https://www.duarte.com/resources/communication-skills/what-is-data-storytelling/
- https://www.duarte.com/services/executive-presentations/
- Frame current state vs possible future; make the business ask and consequence of action/inaction explicit. Keep audience needs and decisions in focus.

## 5. Storytelling with Data - visual focus

- https://www.storytellingwithdata.com/blog/simplifying-a-gantt-chart
- Reduce clutter, use intentional highlights, focus the viewer's attention on what matters. Use examples as inspiration, not a mandatory visual template.
